# Quality gate

## Scope safety

- Only authorized page, style, script, and product asset files changed.
- Homepage and unrelated product pages still load.
- Existing user changes were preserved.

## Product accuracy

- International name matches the actual product.
- Every shown configuration has a matching real photo or confirmed listing.
- Units and conversions are correctly labeled.
- Domestic codes appear only as factory references.
- No unsupported certification, standard, project, material, pressure, dimension, or performance claim.
- Old artwork containing conflicting material or model information is not presented as evidence.

## Images and interaction

- No broken image or distorted product.
- Visible product and `冠亚` markings are preserved.
- Gallery arrows, thumbnails, zoom, Escape close, and mobile swipe work.
- Mixed aspect ratios do not change the gallery frame height materially.
- Alt text describes the exact product configuration.
- Generated visuals are not presented as real packing, factory, test, certification, or project evidence.

## Conversion

- WhatsApp/RFQ text names the product series.
- Email and navigation links work.
- Buyer is prompted for relevant configuration inputs.
- Page remains usable without relying on text embedded in images.

## Responsive review

- Check at least one desktop and one mobile viewport.
- No horizontal page overflow except intentional thumbnail/table scrolling.
- Main actions and specifications remain readable on mobile.
- Sticky header does not cover anchored headings.
