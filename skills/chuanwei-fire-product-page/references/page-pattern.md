# Product page pattern

Use the smallest structure that fully explains the series.

1. Header consistent with the company website.
2. Hero: international product name, concise factory/OEM position, one clean real product image, core selection facts, inquiry action.
3. Product gallery: one large frame, thumbnails, previous/next controls, zoom, mobile swipe, descriptive captions.
4. Technical overview: a readable definition list or two-column table with international units.
5. Available models: configurations and options, with domestic codes only as factory references.
6. Product-specific OEM/ODM capabilities.
7. Real packing or quality evidence when supplied.
8. Technical inquiry: prefilled product family and useful buyer inputs.
9. Back-to-category navigation; previous/next series navigation is optional and supplementary.

## Design defaults

- Clean industrial B2B presentation: dark navy, white, neutral gray, limited fire red.
- Product photography is the visual focus. Avoid flames, explosions, decorative emergency scenes, and generic badge clutter.
- Keep one dominant heading per section, short explanatory copy, and restrained cards.
- Keep mixed-aspect images within a stable frame; never stretch them.
- Category pages display product families directly. Do not hide primary navigation inside a carousel.
- Product pages may use a carousel or gallery because the images describe one series.

## Data placement

- Put language-independent real photos first.
- Put translatable names, parameters, model options, OEM content, and disclaimers in HTML.
- Use an English infographic only as supporting content. If the website later adds another language, the page must remain understandable without translating every bitmap.
