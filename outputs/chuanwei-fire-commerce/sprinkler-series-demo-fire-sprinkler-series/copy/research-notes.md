# Fire Sprinkler Series terminology research

Access date: 2026-08-15

## Findings used in the prototype

- The current Chinese national standard title is `GB 5135.1-2019 Automatic sprinkler system—Part 1: Sprinklers`. This is retained as research context only and is not presented as a claim for the photographed products.
- `Pendent`, `upright`, and `sidewall` are established sprinkler configuration terms. The local images visibly include these configurations and product markings such as `ZSTZ`, `ZSTX`, and `ZSTBS`.
- K-factor, temperature, response classification, pressure, connection, material, certification, and application suitability vary by model. They remain unpublished pending matching manufacturer data.
