# Wet Alarm Check Valve Assembly

## Buyer-facing summary

Wet alarm check valve assemblies for wet-pipe sprinkler systems, available with flanged or grooved connections. Complete assemblies include the main valve and alarm trim. Connection dimensions, trim configuration, nameplate, logo and coating color can be produced to an approved drawing or sample.
