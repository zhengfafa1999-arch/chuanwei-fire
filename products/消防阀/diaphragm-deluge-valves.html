Deluge-System Water Control Valve

Diaphragm Deluge Valve

A diaphragm-actuated water control valve for open deluge sprinkler systems, offered with flanged or grooved connections. In the standby state the valve holds supply water back; on fire detection or manual release it opens and floods the deluge piping so open sprinklers or nozzles discharge over the protected area.
