Indoor First-Aid Fire Hose Reel

Straight-Stream Fire Hose Reel

A robust fire hose reel with a 19 mm semi-rigid hose and a copper straight-stream nozzle, for first-aid firefighting in indoor hydrant systems. Offered in 0.8, 1.0 and 1.6 MPa pressure classes.
