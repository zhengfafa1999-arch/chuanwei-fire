Coverage comparison

Quick response does not define coverage area

Response type describes how quickly the thermal element operates. Coverage classification describes the design area permitted for the sprinkler. Both values—and the required flow and pressure—must come from the exact product technical data.

QR / Standard Coverage

Quick Response Standard Spray

Fast-response thermal element with standard spray coverage. Common options in our range include pendent, upright and horizontal sidewall styles.

EC / QR

Quick Response Extended Coverage

Also quick response, but developed for a larger design coverage area. Our factory list includes K8.0 (metric 115), 68°C / 155°F upright, pendent and sidewall options; exact coverage, spacing, flow and pressure remain subject to the matching technical sheet.
